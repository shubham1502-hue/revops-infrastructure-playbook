# Outcomes and Results

## Measured Outcomes (Estimated from 14-Month Build)

| Outcome | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales cycle time | ~31 days avg | ~26 days avg | **-15%** |
| Lead-to-demo conversion | ~28% | ~33% | **+5pp** |
| Lead capture delay | 12-24 hours | Real-time (< 1 min) | **Eliminated** |
| Invoice creation delay | 24-72 hours | < 1 minute | **Eliminated** |
| CEO report prep time | 2-3 hours/week | 30 min | **-80%** |
| Manual coordination overhead | ~15 hrs/week (all teams) | ~2 hrs/week | **-87%** |
| Pipeline visibility confidence | Low | High | **Qualitative** |
| Finance-sales reconciliation errors | Frequent | Near zero | **Eliminated** |

---

## How These Numbers Were Produced

### Sales Cycle (15% reduction)
- Baseline: average days from lead creation to Close Won in spreadsheet records for the 6 months before build
- Post-build: average days from CRM lead creation timestamp to close date for 6 months post-build
- Attribution: faster lead response, stricter qualification, stage-gated progression

### Lead → Demo Conversion (+5pp)
- Baseline: estimated from spreadsheet records (demo count / lead count per month)
- Post-build: tracked directly in CRM with stage transition timestamps
- Attribution: real-time lead ingestion + SLA enforcement reduced early-stage drop-off

### Time Savings
- Estimated through time tracking conversations with reps, ops, and finance during the baseline audit
- Not precisely measured - directional estimate only

---

## What the System Made Possible That Didn't Exist Before

1. **Source-level cohort analysis** - ability to compare close rates and deal values by acquisition channel over time
2. **Stage-level conversion tracking** - knowing exactly where in the funnel leads were dropping off
3. **Discount behavior analysis** - comparing Locked Proposal Amount to Final Close Amount across reps and time
4. **Real-time pipeline forecast** - weighted pipeline value updated instantly as stage changes occurred
5. **Nightly finance reconciliation** - automatic detection of closed deals without a matching Books invoice

---

## Most Important Single Impact

The most important outcome was not any individual metric. It was that the CEO could open a dashboard on Friday morning and trust what it said - without asking anyone to verify it first.

That shift in confidence is what transforms operations from a reporting exercise into a decision-making tool.

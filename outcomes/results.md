# Operating Changes and Measurement

## Before and after

| Workflow | Starting point | Change supported by the team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead intake | Enquiries reached the team through several paths. | Capture and routing followed a common workflow with a next owner. |
| Pipeline | Stage definitions and deal information varied between records. | CRM stages, required fields, and owners gave the team a shared view of open work. |
| Handoff | Receiving teams assembled context from manual updates. | Handoff rules defined which fields and responsibilities moved to operations and service. |
| Reporting | Leadership updates depended on reconciling multiple inputs. | The pipeline review used consistent stage definitions and surfaced records that needed follow-up. |

My work covered the CRM migration, workflow configuration, handoff design with the teams involved, and leadership reporting. Sales, operations, and service teams owned execution and adoption in their workflows.

## How to measure the changes

| Measure | Definition | Checks before comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales cycle | Elapsed time from lead creation to Closed Won for won deals with both events. | Record cohort dates, deal types, counts, excluded deals, and timestamp availability. Compare periods with similar mix and seasonality. |
| Lead-to-demo | Unique eligible leads with a completed demo divided by unique eligible leads in the same creation cohort. | Record the eligibility rule and observation window. Track qualified-to-demo separately. |
| Lead intake and response | Submission to CRM creation, and submission to the first logged human contact. | Keep both clocks and define how missing or duplicate records are handled. |
| Reporting and coordination effort | Time spent preparing leadership reporting and time spent on cross-team follow-up, tracked as distinct activities. | Use consistent task and team boundaries and identify estimates from interviews or logs. |
| Finance handoff, if applied | Time from Closed Won to invoice draft, plus the rate of unmatched deal-to-invoice records. | Reconcile by source deal ID, define exposure and exception states, and check that dates represent the same events. |

Use the [baseline audit](baseline-audit.md) to define the starting workflow and the [metrics spec](../templates/sales-ops-metrics-spec.md) to agree on grain and denominators. Record concurrent changes and distinguish an observed team-level change from an individual contribution.

# Baseline Audit

In the original workflow, sales, operations, and service teams drew on CRM records, spreadsheets, and manual updates. The baseline audit identifies where ownership, stage definitions, and handoff evidence were unclear.

| Workflow | Starting condition | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead capture | Lead information arrived across several paths with uneven field completeness. | Source submissions, CRM creation timestamps, assigned owners, and first human contact. |
| Pipeline | Stage labels and required fields varied across teams and records. | Field and stage maps, stage events, and records with missing or inconsistent definitions. |
| Handoffs | Teams relied on manual messages to share context and request action. | Required fields, timestamps, acknowledgements, exception owners, and rework examples. |
| Leadership reporting | Pipeline updates required assembly across sources. | Report preparation steps, time logs or documented estimates, definitions, and unresolved discrepancies. |
| Finance coordination | The finance handoff is a separate integration design question. | For that design, inspect the sales-to-invoice reference, draft status, matching exceptions, and owner. |

Record the comparison cohort and time window before measuring. Use one row per lead or deal where applicable, with explicit inclusion rules for time and conversion metrics. Document assumptions and gaps in historical evidence. [Operating changes and measurement](results.md)
